DopplerIQ Support

Need help, found a bug, or have an idea? Email cmrutherford3@gmail.com. Include your device (iPhone, iPad or Mac) and what you were doing when the problem happened. A screenshot helps a lot.

Common questions

The radar or forecast won't load. Check your internet connection, then pull down to refresh or close and reopen the app. Weather services occasionally go down for a few minutes.

It's showing the wrong location. Make sure location access is allowed in Settings, Privacy & Security, Location Services, DopplerIQ. You can also search for any city or ZIP code.

I subscribed but features are still locked. Open Settings in the app and tap Restore Purchases. Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple account you used to subscribe.

How do I cancel? On iPhone or iPad: Settings, tap your name, Subscriptions, DopplerIQ. On Mac: App Store, your account, Subscriptions. Cancelling stops the next renewal; you keep paid features until the end of the current period.

Does one subscription work on all my devices? Yes. One subscription covers DopplerIQ on iPhone, iPad and Mac with the same Apple account.

Where does the data come from? Mostly the National Weather Service and NOAA, plus Open-Meteo forecast models. DopplerIQ is not for navigation or life-safety decisions; always follow official warnings.
