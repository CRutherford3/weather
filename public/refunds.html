DopplerIQ Refund Policy

Last updated November 24, 2026

Short version: if you're not happy, email within 7 days of a charge and you'll get a refund. You can cancel any time and keep your plan until the end of the period you paid for.

Subscriptions (Plus and Pro)

  • 7-day refund: email cmrutherford3@gmail.com within 7 days of a new subscription or renewal charge, and that charge will be refunded in full.
  • After 7 days: charges aren't refunded, but you can cancel any time and keep your plan until the end of the period you already paid for. It won't renew again.
  • Free trials: cancel before the trial ends and you won't be charged. Forgot to cancel? Email within 7 days of the charge for a refund.

Pro Radar Pack

Email within 7 days of buying the Radar Pack for a full refund. Refunded purchases are removed from your account.

Charged by mistake

Double charges or charges you didn't expect will always be refunded. Just email.

App Store purchases

Purchases made inside the iPhone, iPad or Mac app are billed by Apple, and only Apple can refund them. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

How to ask

Email cmrutherford3@gmail.com from the email on your DopplerIQ account, or include it in your message. Refunds go back to your original payment method through Stripe and usually show up within 5 to 10 business days.
