DopplerIQ Privacy Policy

Last updated November 10, 2026

Short version: DopplerIQ has no accounts, no analytics and no tracking. Your location and settings stay on your device. To show you the weather, the app sends map coordinates to public weather services.

Who runs DopplerIQ

DopplerIQ is an independent app made by Charles Rutherford. If you have a question about this policy, email cmrutherford3@gmail.com.

Location

If you allow it, the app uses your location while it is open to show radar, alerts and the forecast where you are. It does not use your location in the background. You can turn location off at any time in your device settings and search for places instead.

What gets sent to weather services

To load weather for a spot, the app sends that spot's coordinates (or the place name you searched) to the services that provide the data. These requests do not include your name, an account or any ID that identifies you. The main services are:

  • National Weather Service and other NOAA offices (forecasts, alerts, radar, satellite, marine and tropical data)
  • Open-Meteo (forecast models and place search)
  • Iowa Environmental Mesonet (radar and warning maps)
  • The DopplerIQ website, which passes a few requests along to weather feeds that apps cannot read directly

Each of these services has its own privacy policy and may keep normal server logs, like any website you visit.

What stays on your device

Saved places, units, map layers and other settings are stored only on your device. I do not have a server that stores them. Deleting the app deletes them.

Purchases

Subscriptions are handled entirely by Apple. I never see your name, email or payment details. Apple tells the app only which plan is active. You can manage or cancel a subscription in your Apple account settings.

Ads

The free plan may show ads. Right now those are DopplerIQ's own ads and collect nothing. If a third-party ad network such as Google AdMob is added later, this policy will be updated first, and the app will ask for your permission before any tracking as Apple requires. Paid plans have no ads.

Website

The DopplerIQ website works the same way: settings are kept in your browser, and the free plan may show ads from Google AdSense, which can use cookies to choose ads.

Children

DopplerIQ is a general weather app and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
